Our people

Built around how the work actually happens.

We are based in Thoothukudi and work across Tamil Nadu. On most interior jobs, things go wrong between one trade and the next. We stay on the job until the handover is done.

  • Materials you can compare

    We bring PVC, MDF, plywood, UPVC, aluminium and glass samples to you. You can see what each one costs and where it works best before you decide.

  • Measured before it is quoted

    We visit the site before quoting. Levels, corners, plumbing and electrical points all get checked, so the price is based on your actual room.

  • One team across trades

    Ceilings, cupboards, windows, blinds and flooring are planned together. The carpenter is not waiting on the electrician, and nothing gets fitted twice.

  • Chosen for this climate

    Salt air, humidity and strong summer sun affect every material. We use UPVC, aluminium and moisture-resistant boards where wood would swell or fade.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before a first site visit.

Still unsure? Call +91 97150 32513 — we are happy to talk it through before you commit to anything.

Which areas around Thoothukudi and Tamil Nadu do you work in?

Our office is at No. 2H/702A, Annai Theresa Nagar, Millerpuram, Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) 628008. We carry out site visits and installations across Thoothukudi, Tiruchendur, Kovilpatti, Tirunelveli, Nagercoil, Madurai and anywhere in Tamil Nadu.

How do I get a price or book a free site visit?

Call or WhatsApp +91 97150 32513 with the rooms you want to work on, or submit the contact form. We visit your site, take exact measurements, bring material samples and give you a free itemised quotation separating material and labour.

Which interior materials last best in Thoothukudi's coastal humidity?

Salt air and humidity affect untreated wood and cheap hardware. For kitchens, utility zones and coastal openings, we recommend PVC or UPVC cupboards, powder-coated aluminium, multi-chamber steel-reinforced UPVC windows, waterproof vinyl flooring and marine-grade stainless steel glass fittings.

Do you supply materials or work with what I have?

Both. Most customers ask us to supply and fit everything so measurements and warranties stay with one team. We also fit blinds, mosquito nets, windows or flooring that you have already bought.

How long does a modular kitchen or false ceiling take?

Timelines depend on the room size, material selection and site readiness. Once measurements are finalised, we provide a written room-by-room schedule with your quotation and keep you updated until handover.

Do you handle offices, clinics and commercial spaces?

Yes. We regularly install aluminium and 12 mm toughened glass partitions, grid ceilings, roller or zebra blinds and vinyl flooring for offices, clinics and retail shops, including after-hours fitting when needed.

Can you take up a single room or small renovation?

Yes. One modular kitchen, a bedroom wardrobe, a balcony glass railing, a set of blinds or window mosquito nets is a regular job for our team.
