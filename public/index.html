Our people

Built around how the work actually happens.

We are based in Thoothukudi and work across Tamil Nadu. On most interior jobs, things go wrong between one trade and the next. We stay on the job until the handover is done.

  • Materials you can compare

    We bring PVC, MDF, plywood, UPVC, aluminium and glass samples to you. You can see what each one costs and where it works best before you decide.

  • Measured before it is quoted

    We visit the site before quoting. Levels, corners, plumbing and electrical points all get checked, so the price is based on your actual room.

  • One team across trades

    Ceilings, cupboards, windows, blinds and flooring are planned together. The carpenter is not waiting on the electrician, and nothing gets fitted twice.

  • Chosen for this climate

    Salt air, humidity and strong summer sun affect every material. We use UPVC, aluminium and moisture-resistant boards where wood would swell or fade.

How we work

Four steps, start to finish.

Every job follows the same order. You will know what happens next, and what it costs, before we start.

  1. 01

    Tell us about the space

    Call, WhatsApp or send the form with the rooms involved and roughly what you have in mind. Photos help.

  2. 02

    Site visit and measurement

    We measure on site, check services and existing finishes, and flag anything that will affect the design or the budget.

  3. 03

    Quotation and material choice

    You get an itemised quote with the material options for each line, so you can see what changes the price.

  4. 04

    Execution and handover

    Work is scheduled room by room, cleaned up as we go, and walked through with you before handover.

FAQ

Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before a first site visit.

Still unsure? Call +91 97150 32513 — we are happy to talk it through before you commit to anything.

Which areas do you work in?

Our office is in Annai Theresa Nagar, Thoothukudi. We work in Thoothukudi and travel to homes, offices and shops across Tamil Nadu. Send us your location and we will tell you when we can visit.

How do I get a price?

Send the rooms you want to change, or call and describe them. We visit, measure and give you an itemised quote that separates material and labour.

Do you supply materials or work with what I have?

Both. Most customers ask us to supply and fit everything. We also fit blinds, nets, windows or flooring that you have already bought.

How long does a modular kitchen take?

It depends on the size, the material and how ready the site is. Once measurements are final, we give you a written timeline with the quote and update you as the work moves.

Do you handle offices and commercial spaces?

Yes. A lot of our aluminium, glass partition and grid ceiling work is for offices, clinics and shops. We can work after hours if the business needs to stay open.

Can you handle a single room?

Yes. One bedroom, a kitchen, a balcony railing or a set of blinds is a normal job for us. We take small jobs.
